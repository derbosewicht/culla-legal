Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Culla does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Your photos stay on your device

Culla reads your photo library only to show you photos and videos for review, to suggest likely junk and documents, and to delete the items you confirm. Photos, videos, and their metadata are never uploaded, copied off your device, or sent to us or anyone else.

On-device analysis

To suggest blurry, dark, or blank photos and receipts, Culla uses Apple's built-in Vision framework on your iPhone. No photo or result ever leaves the device, and iCloud-only photos are not downloaded for analysis.

What is stored on your device

Culla keeps small files with your review progress (which items you've reviewed or marked for deletion and how much space you've freed), your levels and badges, and scan results. These stay on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking

Culla has no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment information. Apple's privacy policy covers those transactions.

Children

Culla does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
