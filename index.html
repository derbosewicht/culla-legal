Support

Where are deleted photos? Culla moves them to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Restore a subscription: open Culla › Settings › Restore Purchases.

Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Culla.

Something else? Email support@metrix.to.
